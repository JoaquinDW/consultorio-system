How it works

An appointment from start to finish, without anyone picking up the phone.

  1. Patient

    Messages on WhatsApp

    They message your clinic’s number at any time. The bot recognizes existing patients by their phone number.

  2. Bot

    Offers available times

    It asks for their name and the service they need, then finds a slot within your opening hours without overlapping appointments.

  3. Schedule

    Books the appointment

    The appointment appears in the day’s schedule with its service, practitioner, and Pending status.

  4. Reminder

    Confirms attendance

    The patient receives a reminder before their appointment. They reply “yes” and its status changes to Confirmed.

You’re in control

The bot handles routine tasks. Your team handles what matters.

Each conversation is in Bot or Human mode. When something needs a person, the bot hands the conversation to your team and flags it for attention.

Handled by the bot

Bot mode · 24 hours a day
  • Replies instantly, at any time, every day.
  • Asks for the patient’s name and service and recognizes your existing patients.
  • Offers available times and books appointments, when enabled in Settings.
  • Sends reminders and confirms the appointment when the patient replies “yes”.
  • Answers administrative questions using the clinic information you provide.

Handled by your team

Human mode · when needed
  • Rescheduling and cancellations. The bot leaves existing bookings unchanged and flags the request for your team.
  • Questions the bot cannot answer, flagged as “Needs attention”.
  • Any conversation you want to take over: switch it to Human mode with one click.
  • Deciding what the bot can do: opening hours, services, and whether it books appointments automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

How much does it cost?

Our current launch offer is ARS 20,000–50,000 per month, depending on usage (practitioners, locations, and message volume). Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll give you a quote for your clinic.

Do I need the official WhatsApp API or a separate provider?

No. The bot connects to your clinic’s WhatsApp by scanning a QR code, just like WhatsApp Web. We recommend using a dedicated clinic number.

Can the bot make a booking mistake?

It only offers available slots within your opening hours, without overlapping appointments. Bookings start as Pending, just like manually entered appointments. If anything is unclear, it hands the conversation to a person.

Can I still book appointments manually?

Yes. The schedule works with or without the bot. You can create, reschedule, and cancel appointments from the dashboard, and send reminders with one click.

Does it store patients’ clinical information?

No. SmartTurnos stores contact details and administrative notes. Diagnoses, treatments, and medical records are deliberately excluded.

Does it support multiple practitioners or locations?

Yes. Each practitioner has their own schedule and opening hours, which can vary by location. Your front desk can see everything; administrators manage the settings.
